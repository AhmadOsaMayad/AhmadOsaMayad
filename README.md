### 🔒 A7 Extractor — *Select. Extract. Done.*

> Pull the exact pages you need out of any PDF — right on your phone.
> **No internet · No accounts · No uploads. Ever.**

<div align="center">
  <img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/AhmadOsaMayad/a7-extractor-showcase/main/screenshots/home-en-light.png" width="21%" alt="A7 Extractor home screen — light theme">
  &nbsp;&nbsp;
  <img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/AhmadOsaMayad/a7-extractor-showcase/main/screenshots/home-en-dark.png" width="21%" alt="A7 Extractor home screen — dark theme">
  &nbsp;&nbsp;
  <img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/AhmadOsaMayad/a7-extractor-showcase/main/screenshots/selection-screen-range-selction-en-dark.png" width="21%" alt="Contiguous range page selection across a PDF">
  &nbsp;&nbsp;
  <img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/AhmadOsaMayad/a7-extractor-showcase/main/screenshots/settings-ar-light.png" width="21%" alt="A7 Extractor settings in Arabic — full RTL layout">
  <br>
  <sub>Home · dark theme · range selection · full Arabic RTL — captured on a real device.</sub>
</div>

- **🔒 Truly offline** — declares *zero* permissions: no `INTERNET`, no storage, no location. Your documents physically cannot leave the device.
- **🎯 Precision selection** — contiguous ranges, arbitrary page sets, or select-all — with every page rendered live before you extract.
- **🧾 Faithful output** — extracted PDFs keep each page's original size and orientation, even in mixed portrait/landscape documents.
- **🌍 Built for both languages** — complete English/Arabic localization, RTL layouts, light and dark themes.

### 📖 Free Dictionary — *the offline dictionary that explains words like a person would*

> **108,000+ entries · 14,800+ idioms · 36,000+ IPA transcriptions** — all shipped inside the APK.
> Infinite, instant, private lookup — on a plane, in a basement, in a dead zone.

<div align="center">
  <img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/AhmadOsaMayad/free-dict-showcase/main/screenshots/search-light.png" width="21%" alt="Free Dictionary search — light theme">
  &nbsp;&nbsp;
  <img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/AhmadOsaMayad/free-dict-showcase/main/screenshots/word-details-view-midside-onidiom-chip-dark.png" width="21%" alt="Idioms and comparisons for a word — dark theme">
  &nbsp;&nbsp;
  <img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/AhmadOsaMayad/free-dict-showcase/main/screenshots/word-details-view-upperside-after-faved-and-saved-light.png" width="21%" alt="Favoriting a word and saving it to a collection">
  &nbsp;&nbsp;
  <img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/AhmadOsaMayad/free-dict-showcase/main/screenshots/library-view-collections-tab-selection-mode-movetonewcollection-light.png" width="21%" alt="Named collections in the library">
  <br>
  <sub>Search · idioms with real examples · favorites &amp; collections · light and dark.</sub>
</div>

- **🧠 Explains, not just defines** — idioms with examples, collocations, phrasal verbs, and confusables, grouped by part of speech.
- **🗣️ Pronunciation with depth** — IPA with US/UK accents and homographs (*read* /ɹid/ vs /ɹɛd/, *bass*, *tear*, *wind*) mapped to their meanings.
- **🔗 Relations that actually resolve** — every synonym chip opens a real entry; 30,000+ broken references were programmatically pruned.
- **📚 Keep what matters** — favorites plus named collections, with multi-select and move-between-collections flows.

### 📸 ANstagram — *a full social product, engineered like one*

> An Instagram-inspired social experience built with **Flutter, Firebase and Clean Architecture** — auth, feed, stories, comments, notifications and direct messages in one cohesive, dark-first app.

<div align="center">
  <img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/AhmadOsaMayad/anstagram-showcase/main/docs/images/27-feed-story-rail.jpg" width="21%" alt="ANstagram home feed with the stories rail">
  &nbsp;&nbsp;
  <img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/AhmadOsaMayad/anstagram-showcase/main/docs/images/20-auth-login.jpg" width="21%" alt="ANstagram sign-in experience">
  &nbsp;&nbsp;
  <img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/AhmadOsaMayad/anstagram-showcase/main/docs/images/29-direct-message-thread.jpg" width="21%" alt="ANstagram direct message thread">
  &nbsp;&nbsp;
  <img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/AhmadOsaMayad/anstagram-showcase/main/docs/images/05-notifications-activity-feed.jpg" width="21%" alt="ANstagram notification activity feed">
  <br>
  <sub>Feed &amp; stories · authentication · direct messages · activity feed.</sub>
</div>

- **🏗️ Architecture that scales** — feature-first `data · domain · presentation` boundaries, DI with `get_it`/`injectable`, functional results with `dartz`.
- **💬 The whole social loop** — likes, saves, follows, threaded comments, story reactions and replies, notifications, post sharing with notes, and DMs.
- **🎨 Intentional states** — unified loading, contextual success/error feedback, retry flows and destructive-action confirmations treated as product, not afterthoughts.
- **🌍 Bilingual by design** — English/Arabic ARB localization, responsive sizing, and a semantic design-token system.
